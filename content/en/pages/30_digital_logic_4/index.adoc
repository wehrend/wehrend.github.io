== How does a CPU work 4 - Table of contents

At the end of part 3, our CPU runs on a real FPGA - but slowly: every instruction needs one long clock cycle,
and the slowest instruction decides how long that is. Most parts of the CPU are idle most of the time.

In part 4 we look at how real processors get faster: first we learn how to measure performance,
then we split the CPU into stages that work at the same time (pipelining) and solve the problems this creates (hazards).
Finally we see how processors execute instructions out of order and even before they know whether they are needed.
