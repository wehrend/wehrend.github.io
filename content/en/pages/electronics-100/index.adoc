= Electronics 101–104 — Overview

This page provides a short overview of the electronics introduction series.

== Electronics 101
link:https://wehrend.github.io/pages/electronics-101/[electronics 101]

Introduction to basic electrical concepts such as current, voltage, resistance, and simple circuits.

== Electronics 102
link:https://wehrend.github.io/pages/electronics-102/[electronics 102]

Covers time-dependent components like capacitors and coils, also simple passive filters.

== Electronics 103
link:https://wehrend.github.io/pages/electronics-103/[electronics 103]

Introduces nonlinear components including diodes, transistors, and their use as switches and amplifiers.

== Electronics 104
link:https://wehrend.github.io/pages/electronics-104/[electronics 104]

Focuses on amplifier circuits, especially integrated amplifiers and operational amplifiers.
